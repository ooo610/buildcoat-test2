Not at all. It’s applied easily with a wide putty knife (not a trowel). It's something every painter or finisher already uses. You can use a Venetian spatula for a smoother surface. However, using a wide putty knife would give you a satisfactory result.

Follow these precise chemical application steps:

  1. Add 25 liters of clean water (50% of the dry weight) to a clean container, then gradually pour in the entire contents of the 50 kg sack while mixing. (Always add powder to water, not the other way around, to avoid lumps).
  2. Mix thoroughly using an electric mixer or paddle until you get a homogeneous, smooth paste with no dry spots.
  3. Use a wide putty knife (30–40 cm) to apply the paste on clean, dry red bricks or block walls.
  4. Spread the material evenly with a 5 mm thickness, which serves as the final layer—no need for additional plaster, primer, or paint.

*Note: No water spraying or curing is required after application. The result is a seamless, modern, acrylic-like wall finish with excellent color uniformity, weather resistance, and a satin sheen.*
