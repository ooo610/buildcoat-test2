About us

Innovation for Every Wall

Progress for Everyone

BuildCoat wasn't born in a boardroom — it was born in a workshop, a lab, and a relentless search for better.

In a market long dominated by low-cost cementitious colored plaster applied manually by craftsmen, one chemistry student from Egypt asked a different question:

"Why not create a smart finishing material that not only replaces traditional layers — plaster, putty, and paint — but optimizes this colored plaster to perform better, last longer, give a unified color with an acrylic-paint look, and have less impact on the environment, making it relatively eco-friendly?"

Buildcoat is the brainchild of a young Egyptian innovator who combined academic knowledge in chemistry with real-world experience in construction.

Driven by a determination to modernize conventional construction practices and eliminate the drawbacks of sub-standard finishings, he identified a vital opportunity to elevate industry standards: while others focused on cheap, he focused on smart — engineering a hybrid gypsum-cement system enhanced with polymers, oxides, and sustainable additives.

While the market accepted patchy colors, he created a finish that's smooth, unified, modern, and dirt-resistant.

"Why hasn't this been done before, despite the regional market demand?"

Traditional colored plaster has remained largely unchanged for decades, relying on basic cementitious mixes. While chemical formulation in construction materials has advanced rapidly, bringing this technology to site-ready coatings required dedicated R&D.

BuildCoat Eco represents the next generation of colored plaster — bridging advanced polymer chemistry with high-durability finishings to replace outdated methods with a single, intelligent system.

Our Core Belief

You don't need to sacrifice: aesthetics, durability, or environmental responsibility to get an affordable wall finish.

Buildcoat proves you can have all three. This isn't just a finish. It's a statement.

Our Vision

To redefine exterior wall finishing in Egypt and the region by introducing an all-in-one solution combining beauty, durability, and affordability.

Meet our founders

Saif Essam

Founder & CEO – BuildCoat

Saif Essam is a chemist and entrepreneur. Since his first year at the Faculty of Science, Capital University, he has focused on developing smarter, more sustainable alternatives to traditional wall finishes.

Through BuildCoat, he blends science, design, and strategy to deliver high-performance materials tailored for emerging markets. His vision goes beyond product innovation — he's also developing a vertically integrated model to control quality, reduce cost, and scale impact across the construction industry.

Abdulrahman Essam

Co-Founder, Digital Marketing & Sales Specialist – BuildCoat

Abdulrahman Essam holds a Bachelor's degree in Digital Marketing from Thebes Academy. With a strong background in digital strategy and brand communication, he plays a key role in shaping BuildCoat's online presence and driving its market outreach.

His work focuses on connecting the brand with its audience through creative storytelling, data-driven campaigns, and performance marketing that supports the company's growth across both B2B and B2C sectors.
